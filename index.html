COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to see results on LinkedIn?
Most clients see noticeable engagement increases within 3-4 weeks of consistent, strategic posting. Significant growth in followers and inbound typically happens within 60-90 days. The timeline depends on your starting point, consistency, and audience targeting.
Do you ghostwrite all my content?
No. I work collaboratively with you. We develop your strategy and voice framework together, and I script content based on your experiences and ideas. You review and approve everything before posting. Your LinkedIn should sound like you.
What if I'm a complete beginner on LinkedIn?
Perfect. That's where many of my clients start. We begin with a foundational audit, optimize your profile completely, establish your positioning, and then build a content strategy specifically designed for beginners. No experience needed.
Can you help if I'm already posting regularly?
Absolutely. If your engagement is stagnant or you feel like you're posting in the dark, we'll audit your content, identify what's not working, realign your strategy, and optimize your approach. Many established posters benefit most from this refresh.
How does the pricing work?
We offer flexible packages based on your needs: strategy-only, content scripting, full management, or hybrid models. Every engagement starts with a ₹1 audit so we understand your specific situation before recommending a package.
What if I'm not happy with the results?
We focus on transparency and real metrics. Every month, you get a detailed performance report. If something isn't working, we adjust immediately. Your success is our metric, and we iterate until we get results.
Can you help me get more inbound clients?
Yes. Inbound generation is one of the core outcomes we focus on. Through positioning, storytelling, and consistent thought leadership, we attract the right audience and make you visible to decision-makers in your space.
How much time will this take from me?
For strategy and content scripting, minimal input from you once we've set direction. For active management, you're mainly approving content (15-20 minutes per week). The entire point is to remove LinkedIn friction from your week.
